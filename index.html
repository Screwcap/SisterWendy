📖 Horse Race Dominoes Rules

🎮 How to Play a Tile

  • Tap/Click any tile in your hand to select it
  • If it fits one end → it plays automatically
  • If it fits both ends → choose LEFT or RIGHT
  • Green glow = playable tiles

🎯 Goal

Be the first to reach 61 points. Score by making the open ends add up to a multiple of 5.

💰 Horse Race Scoring

After each play, add the two open ends of the chain:

  • Ends total 5 → Score 5 points
  • Ends total 10 → Score 10 points
  • Ends total 15 → Score 15 points
  • Ends total 20 → Score 20 points

🎲 Special Rules for Doubles

  • Doubles count BOTH pips — a [5|5] on the end = 10 points, not 5!
  • Playing a double = bonus turn — you play again!
  • Scoring = bonus turn — keep playing while you're hot!

📋 Other Rules

  • Draw if stuck: Can't play? Draw from the boneyard until you can (or it's empty)
  • Domino! Empty your hand first to win the round and score opponent's remaining pips (rounded to nearest 5)
  • Blocked game: If nobody can play, lowest pip count wins
