📖 How to Play Dominoes

🎯 Goal

Be the first to reach 61 points. Score by making the ends of the chain add up to a multiple of 5.

🎲 Basic Rules

  • Match the ends: Play a tile by matching one of its numbers to an open end of the chain.
  • Draw if stuck: If you can't play, draw from the boneyard until you can (or it's empty).
  • Domino! Empty your hand first to win the round and score your opponent's remaining pips.

💰 Scoring (Horse Race)

After each play, add the two open ends:

  • Ends = 5 → Score 5 points
  • Ends = 10 → Score 10 points
  • Ends = 15 → Score 15 points
  • Ends = 20 → Score 20 points

When you score, you get another turn!
