## 1. Domino Dreams (SuperPlay / Playtika, acquired ~$700M)

### What It Actually Is
Solitaire-style game with a domino skin — not traditional chain dominoes. The gameplay is matching and placement puzzles, not the All-Fives variant Sister Wendy plays. Different game entirely, but the monetisation and growth mechanics are directly applicable.

### Monetisation Model
- Free to play, aggressive IAP (coins, boosters, extra lives)
- Key tactic: no IAP offers until Level 22 — deliberate "slow ask" strategy. Players bond with the game before being shown a purchase screen.
- FBFB mechanic (Free Bundle First Bundle): player cannot leave the offer screen until they collect a free item, creating curiosity and commitment to the purchasing flow. No exit option.
- Live events and collectible albums drive recurring engagement and seasonal IAP spikes.
- Acquired by Playtika 2024 — validates the model.

### What Players Love
- Narrative and character progression (recurring characters they root for)
- Visual quality — polished, colourful
- Daily rewards and streaks

### What Players Hate
- Coin drain — energy/progress systems designed to run out at key moments
- 20+ popup launches before you can play
- Suspected rigged mechanics: negative reviews repeatedly allege the game gets harder when coin balance drops
- Pay-to-progress walls

### What Sister Wendy Can Steal
- The "slow ask" — do not show monetisation until the player has bonded with Wendy (at least 5 games played)
- Narrative investment: Wendy's character is already strong; lean into her story across sessions
- Seasonal theming: a "Wendy's Advent Dominoes" or "Easter Retreat" challenge costs nothing and drives return visits

### Sister Wendy's Edge
- Actual dominoes — the real game, not a reskin
- No dark patterns — instant play, no popups, no coin drain, no pay-to-progress
- Character authenticity — Wendy's dialogue is 100+ lines of genuine personality; Domino Dreams characters are marketing constructs
- This is a real positioning advantage: the honest game vs the extractive one

## 2. Dominoes Gold (Skillz platform)

### What It Actually Is
Real-money skill tournament platform. Players enter cash tournaments; top performers win prizes.

### Monetisation Model
- Entry fees for tournaments (real money)
- Skill-based gambling where legal
- 4.7 stars App Store (players like the competitive format)
- 1.4 stars Trustpilot — brutal reviews from players with withdrawal problems, suspected bots, account issues

### What Players Love
- Real money staking creates genuine stakes and engagement
- Leaderboard competition motivates skilled players

### What Players Hate
- 4-6 week payout delays (most common complaint)
- Suspected bot opponents despite "real players" marketing
- Jurisdiction blocking (many US states, all of Canada/UK excluded)
- Glitch-triggered entry fee losses (game crashes, stake lost)

### What Sister Wendy Can Steal
- Friend challenge links — the ability to challenge a specific person is highly valued in reviews; costs nothing in a browser game
- Competitive streak-based bragging (Sister Wendy already has streaks; surface them harder in share text)

### Sister Wendy's Edge
- No account required — instant play beats any friction in the casual segment
- No money at risk — pure entertainment, no legal exposure
- Trust — the contrast with Skillz's Trustpilot disaster is a marketing opportunity: "Play dominoes. No entry fees. No bots. No waiting 6 weeks for your winnings."

## 3. VIP Dominoes / Domino Duel (Zariba)

### What It Actually Is
Traditional chain dominoes against online opponents. One of the more authentic domino experiences on mobile.

### Monetisation Model
- ~$9.99/month subscription removes ads
- Aggressive ad-before-subscribe funnel (interstitial after every game)
- Challenge link mechanic: share a game link to invite a specific opponent (drives viral installs)

### What Players Love
- Actual dominoes rules
- Challenge links — social play mechanic
- Multiplayer with real people (when matchmaking works)

### What Players Hate
- Bot complaints despite "real players" claims
- Aggressive ad frequency: "an ad after every single game"
- Subscription felt like a "ransom" for basic gameplay

### What Sister Wendy Can Steal
- The challenge link mechanic — allow a player to share a link that starts a specific game seed, turning the daily challenge into a synchronous competition with a friend
- Seasonal leaderboards — even without real multiplayer, a weekly leaderboard (streak-based) creates competitive tension

### Sister Wendy's Edge
- Single player, always works — no matchmaking failures, no bots, no waiting
- No subscription pressure — experience is not degraded without paying
- Character — a nun opponent with personality beats anonymous online opponents in the casual segment

## 4. Dominoes+ (MobilityWare)

### What It Actually Is
The volume market leader: 220,000+ ratings on App Store, one of the most-downloaded domino apps in the Western market.

### Monetisation Model
- Retroactively applied subscription to users who had made one-time purchases — generated a class action lawsuit and significant brand damage
- Current model: subscription removes ads
- Multiple AI difficulty levels with distinct personalities are the most praised feature

### What Players Love
- AI personality differentiation — reviewers consistently mention "I like playing against [character name]" — the distinct opponent personalities are the number one driver of positive sentiment
- Game variety (multiple domino variants)
- Smooth, reliable gameplay

### What Players Hate
- The subscription betrayal — one-time purchase bait-and-switch destroyed long-term trust with loyal users
- "Felt like being cheated" (re: retroactive subscription)
- AI perceived as cheating at higher difficulties: "it draws the exact tile it needs every time"

### What Sister Wendy Can Steal
- AI opponent personality differentiation is validated as the primary retention driver — Sister Wendy's single character is actually weaker than MobilityWare's multi-character model. Adding 1-2 more nun characters (a nervous novice, an imperious abbess) would add variety.
- Multiple named difficulty levels with narrative flavour — Sister Wendy already has this (Forgiving / Focused / Merciless). Keep it. Emphasise it on first visit.

### Sister Wendy's Edge
- No subscription betrayal risk
- Brand authenticity — one iconic character beats six forgettable ones
- No AI cheating perception — Wendy's AI is documented and built fairly

## Cross-Competitor Patterns — What the Market Punishes

1. Bot deception — promising real players but delivering bots destroys trust permanently (Dominoes Gold, VIP Dominoes)
2. Subscription bait-and-switch — retroactive pricing changes create class action exposure (Dominoes+)
3. Excessive monetisation friction — popup overload is the most common 1-star complaint category across all apps
4. Pay-to-progress walls — perceived as rigging; drives 1-star reviews even from otherwise happy players

Sister Wendy has none of these problems by design. This is a genuine competitive moat.

---

## Cross-Competitor Patterns — What the Market Rewards

1. Character and personality — the most-praised feature across all apps is distinct AI opponent personality
2. Daily challenges — drives daily return visits better than any other single mechanic
3. Challenge links — friend vs friend social mechanic drives viral installs
4. Instant play — no login, no setup, no account beats friction every time in the casual segment

Sister Wendy already has 1, 2, and 4. Challenge links (3) are the single most implementable gap.
